# FermiLink Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 12, 2026

## Summary First

- FermiLink processes account data, prompts, messages, files, and generated artifacts to operate the service.
- Per-session computational data is stored in workspace directories.
- Workspace data can be deleted for storage, security, or operational reasons.
- Third-party AI/model infrastructure is used to process prompts and generate responses.
- We do not sell personal information.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account and Authentication Data

When login/signup is enabled, we may collect:

- username/email identifier
- password hash (not plaintext password)
- account timestamps
- user group/quota metadata

### 1.2 Chat and Request Data

When you use FermiLink, we process and may store:

- prompts and chat messages
- assistant responses
- thread/session metadata

### 1.3 Workspace and Artifact Data

For each session, FermiLink may create data under workspace roots (for example
`FERMILINK_WORKSPACES_ROOT/<session_id>/`) including:

- generated code/scripts/configuration files
- logs and command outputs
- simulation outputs and plots
- package overlay manifest data
- uploaded/generated artifacts attached in chat

### 1.4 Usage and Quota Data

To enforce daily usage limits, we may store per-user counters and timestamps.

### 1.5 Technical and Security Data

We may process technical metadata and logs needed for reliability, abuse
prevention, and security operations.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- authenticate users and manage accounts
- route prompts to suitable scientific package contexts
- execute requests and stream model/tool outputs
- store/display chat history and artifacts
- enforce quotas and abuse controls
- maintain and improve service reliability

## 3. Automated Routing and Processing

FermiLink can automatically analyze prompt text to choose a scientific package
(keyword routing plus model-based second checks). This is automated processing
used to improve response relevance.

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure

We may disclose information:

- to model/AI service providers needed to process prompts
- to infrastructure providers acting on our behalf
- when required by law or legal process
- to protect rights, security, and platform integrity

We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Data Retention

Retention depends on operational, security, and capacity requirements.

Key points:

- Workspace/session data is not guaranteed to persist.
- Workspace data may be deleted for cleanup, capacity constraints, inactivity, or policy/security reasons.
- Deleted data may be unrecoverable.

You are responsible for exporting important results promptly.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards for this class of service, but no system is
perfectly secure and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 7. International Processing

Depending on deployment and providers, information may be processed in
jurisdictions with different data-protection rules.

## 8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction,
or deletion of personal data. Operators may need to verify identity and may
retain certain data where legally required.

## 9. Children's Privacy

FermiLink is not intended for children under 13 (or minimum required age in
your jurisdiction).

## 10. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date at top indicates the current
version.

## 11. Contact

For privacy requests, contact the service operator.
